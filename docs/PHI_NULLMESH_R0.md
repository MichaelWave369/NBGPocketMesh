# ΦNullMesh R0 — Finite boundary algebra

An isolated mathematical laboratory in NBGPocketMesh, motivated by Venkatesa Chandrasekaran, *An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone*, arXiv:2611.34924v1 (https://arxiv.org/html/2611.34924v1), especially sections 2.2, 3.6 and appendix E. The preprint concerns gravitational constraints, Hilbert-space representations and gluing amplitudes; R0 implements none of those physical derivations. No claim of JT gravity reproduction, C*-algebra completion, spacetime quantization or physical holography.

## Exact mathematical contract

Boundary = (id, frame, dimension, tick). Dimension 1–3, tick 0–99. A pocket is a linear map from the input space to the output space, represented by an output-dimension × input-dimension matrix with Gaussian-integer coefficients (real/imaginary pairs). Directed tick order nondecreasing, equal tick only for identical boundary. This is an artificial ordering constraint, not a relativistic causal structure. Gluing A then B requires exact equality of A.output and B.input, including frame, id, dimension and tick, and yields B A. Identity maps preserve boundary; reverse-time or mismatched interfaces refuse. No implicit frame conversion or permissions. No native mesh execution or Altermath opcode changes.

Gaussian integers form a ring, not the complex field. Matrix algebra over the ring is associative and distributive; the adjoint reverses order. Implementation limits coefficients to magnitude 1,000,000 per component, dimensions to 3, and rejects out-of-range outputs. Under these bounds each ordinary two-factor intermediate is at most 6e12, exact in JS safe integer arithmetic. The three-factor direct contraction is only a small-fixture oracle: intermediate numbers must stay safe; it is not a general unbounded arithmetic implementation. Bounded representation is **not** closed under all ring operations; overflow is intentionally refused. No floating-point approximations to complex values. General positivity of M†M follows from v†M†Mv = ||Mv||² >=0; test checks finite probes, not an exhaustive proof. Unitary norm conservation is conditional on U†U=I. Nonunitary maps need not conserve norm. Trace cyclic for compatible square matrices, not arbitrary reordering of maps.
